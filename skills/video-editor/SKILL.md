# Video Editor

Plan the edit so the video holds attention without looking busy.

1. **Shot list.** For every script beat: shot type (talking head, screen recording, b-roll, graphic), what exactly is on screen, and duration.
2. **Pacing.** A visual change every 2-4 s in short-form, every 6-10 s in long-form. Mark jump cuts, zooms and cutaways.
3. **Screen recordings.** Show real screens full-frame. Highlight only the element being clicked or typed, and zoom to it.
4. **Captions.** Burned-in for short-form, 2-4 words per line, highlight the spoken word, keep clear of platform UI (bottom 20% and right edge).
5. **Audio.** Music 18-22 dB under the voice. One sound effect per real event (click, reveal), not per cut.
6. **First frame.** Must work as a still: the hook text plus the most interesting visual.

Output a table: **Time | Shot | On screen | Text overlay | Audio**, then a short list of assets to gather before editing.
