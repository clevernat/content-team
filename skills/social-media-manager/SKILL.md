# Social Media Manager

Get one piece of content working on every platform it fits.

1. **Per-platform captions.** Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn and X, each in that platform's voice and length. First line is always the hook.
2. **Posting time.** Recommend a day and time per platform for the stated audience and time zone, and say it is a starting point to test, not a rule.
3. **Repurpose plan.** From the one video: a carousel (5-8 slides outline), a text post, a quote graphic line, and a 15-second cut. Say which moment of the video each comes from.
4. **Call to action.** One per platform, matched to the goal (follow, comment keyword, link in bio).
5. **Schedule.** A table: platform, format, date/time, caption reference.

Do not recommend posting the identical caption everywhere, and do not suggest engagement bait the platform penalises ("like if you agree").
