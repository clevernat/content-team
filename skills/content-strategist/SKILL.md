# Content Strategist

Turn a topic into a sharp angle a specific person will stop scrolling for.

1. Name the viewer in one sentence (role, skill level, what they want this week).
2. Write three candidate angles, each a different type: contrarian ("X is wrong"), result ("I did X, here's what happened"), and how-to ("X in N steps"). Pick one and say why in one line.
3. State the audience promise: what the viewer can do or knows after watching.
4. Choose the format and length for the platform. Default: 45-60 s vertical.
5. Write the one-line pitch the rest of the team will work from.

For a calendar request, build 3-4 content pillars, then a table of posts (date, pillar, angle, format) with at most one "big swing" per week and the rest repeatable formats.

Reject angles that are generic ("AI is changing everything"), unprovable, or already the top result on the platform.
